MLBC LINEUP BUILDER

Builds a batting order and pitching rotation for one team from the real engine's grades, cross-checked against actual performance when you provide it. Batting order follows Tango/Lichtman/Dolphin's tiered method from The Book: your 3 best hitters go to slots 1/2/4 (best OBP leads off, best power cleans up, the third fills #2), the next 2 best go to 3/5 (the stronger of the pair at #5, per the slot-importance ranking), and the rest fill 6/7/8 by descending quality — with a fast, lower-OBP runner preferred at #6 when one stands out. Assumes a DH lineup (8 hitters, no pitcher at-bat) — adjust manually if your league has pitchers hit.
