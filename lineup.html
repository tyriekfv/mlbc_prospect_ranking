MLBC LINEUP BUILDER

Builds a batting order and pitching rotation for one team from the real engine's grades, cross-checked against actual performance when you provide it. A hitter's OBP/SLG/OPS are a recency-weighted blend of up to the last 3 years of real stats (whichever years clear a minimum sample — thin partial seasons don't count), falling back to the engine's projected grade only when a player has no qualifying real season on file at all. Batting order follows Tango/Lichtman/Dolphin's tiered method from The Book: your 3 best hitters go to slots 1/2/4 (best OBP leads off, best power cleans up, the third fills #2), the next 2 best go to 3/5 (the stronger of the pair at #5, per the slot-importance ranking), and the rest fill 6/7/8 by descending quality — with a fast, lower-OBP runner preferred at #6 when one stands out. Assumes a DH lineup (8 hitters, no pitcher at-bat) — adjust manually if your league has pitchers hit. Hitters fully trained (1.00 proficiency) at more than one position are automatically evaluated for all of them — anything under 1.00 is excluded from an alternate position entirely, since partial training is a real fielding liability, not a "can pinch-hit there" situation.

Minor-league eligibility

Not on the active roster, but pulled into the pool because they cleared one of the bars above: age, AAA seasons (distinct years in their player card tagged AAA — partial seasons and multi-team years each count once), or a prospect-rating exception (the game's own internal scouting number for this player, read directly from Players.hh, is elite enough to trust despite not clearing the other two).
